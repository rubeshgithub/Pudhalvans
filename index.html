The Northern Lights

The Northern Lights, also known as Aurora Borealis, are one of nature's most spectacular displays. They occur when charged particles from the sun collide with gases in Earth's atmosphere. These collisions create beautiful curtains of light that dance across the night sky in shades of green, pink, red, and purple.

The best places to see the Northern Lights are near the Arctic Circle, including Alaska, Canada, Iceland, and Norway. The lights are most visible during winter months when nights are longest. Many people travel thousands of miles just to witness this incredible natural phenomenon.

🧠 Critical Reading

Argumentative Text: Social Media and Society

Social media has fundamentally transformed how we communicate and interact. Proponents argue that it connects people across vast distances, enables rapid information sharing, and provides platforms for marginalized voices. However, critics contend that social media contributes to mental health issues, spreads misinformation, and creates echo chambers that polarize society. Studies show that excessive social media use correlates with increased anxiety and depression, particularly among teenagers. Yet, the same platforms have been instrumental in organizing social movements and raising awareness about critical issues.
